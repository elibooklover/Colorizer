# Privacy Policy for Colorizer

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Colorizer ("we," "us," or "our") processes information when you use the Colorizer iOS application and its associated online services. Colorizer provides AI-powered photo colorization, optional subscriptions, purchased credits, and rewarded advertising.

## 2. Information We Process

- **Photos:** Images you select and submit are transmitted to our servers for AI processing. The processed image is returned to the App. Photos may contain personal information about you or others. Only submit images you have permission to use.
- **Account and service identifiers:** We process account or session identifiers and authentication information needed to associate requests, purchases, and credit balances with the correct user.
- **Purchases and credits:** We process Apple transaction identifiers, subscription status, purchase history, credit balances, reward verification information, and processing activity.
- **Technical information:** IP addresses, request metadata, device information, error information, and service logs may be processed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the Services.
- **Advertising information:** Google AdMob and its consent SDK may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising data, product interactions, crash information, and performance or diagnostic data.

Apple processes payments. We do not directly receive your full payment card details.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to process photos and deliver results, manage accounts and credits, verify purchases and advertising rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service reliability, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Advertising information may also be used to deliver and measure advertising, subject to your privacy choices and applicable law.

## 4. AI Photo Processing

Photo colorization is performed on remote processing infrastructure rather than exclusively on your device. Submitted images and generated results are processed to fulfill your request. The service does not provide a public photo gallery or social sharing feed. If you share an image outside Colorizer, the receiving service's privacy practices apply.

## 5. Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

Colorizer uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements. Where tracking is used, Colorizer requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before engaging in tracking. You can decline permission or change your choice in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you decline tracking, Colorizer does not use your information for tracking as defined by Apple. You may continue using the App and available credits, and may receive eligible non-tracking rewarded ads where permitted by your consent choices and advertising availability. Tracking permission is not a condition of receiving an otherwise eligible reward.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides additional privacy choices where required, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Where available, you can revisit these choices through the App's privacy settings. ATT permission does not replace any legally required advertising consent.

Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. Third-Party Services and Disclosure

Information may be processed by the following providers as necessary for their respective functions:

- **Apple:** App distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and transaction verification. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Google AdMob and UMP:** Advertising, consent management, and reward verification. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Cloudflare:** Network connectivity, security, and delivery of API requests. [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service security. Advertising-related disclosures are subject to applicable consent requirements and privacy rights.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed. Photo processing may involve temporary copies of uploaded images and generated results. Account, purchase, credit, security, and diagnostic records may be retained as needed to provide the Services, verify transactions, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

You may request information about retention or request deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Certain records may need to be retained where permitted or required by law. Deleting the App does not necessarily delete server-side records or cancel an Apple subscription.

## 8. Security and International Processing

We use HTTPS for communication with the online Services and take reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Our processing infrastructure is located in the United States, and third-party providers may process information in other countries. Applicable legal requirements for international transfers apply.

## 9. Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and opt out of certain advertising-related uses or disclosures. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Where applicable, we process information to perform our agreement with you, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, or on the basis of consent. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise your rights, contact us below. You can also manage tracking permissions in iOS Settings and advertising consent through the App's available privacy controls.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Colorizer is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without legally required parental authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can address the issue.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our Services or legal requirements. We will publish the updated policy with a revised date and provide additional notice where required.
